# PR-08 V31 Right Equipment Density

## Verdict

Accepted-forward only.

V31 switches from the V30 map-stage corridor-mouth work to Slice 5 right-panel
density. The equipment rack now uses a tighter demo-aspect height, smaller
equipment sockets, a wider paper-doll stance, compact armory bay plates behind
slot pairs and fuller authored plates for visual-only empty equipment sockets.

This is not director-grade closure. The right panel is denser and less empty
than V30, but `UI/UI-demo-new.png` still has stronger item art density,
equipment-slot material richness and section polish. The bottom deck and map
stage are intentionally unchanged in this pass.

## Implementation

1. Reduced demo-aspect equipment socket size from `64f` to `58f`.
2. Reduced demo-aspect equipment section ratio from `0.42f` to `0.38f`.
3. Widened equipment column gap while keeping it bounded by panel width.
4. Added compact armory bay plates behind equipment socket pairs.
5. Kept the central shoulder mantle bounded around the paper-doll center so it
   stays a torso/shoulder scaffold instead of a full-width grid bar.
6. Added visual-only authored inner plates so empty equipment targets read as
   deliberate sockets, not tiny glyphs floating in empty squares.

The pass is presentation-only and remains in `client`; labels, hotkeys, item
icons and inventory contents remain runtime/model-owned.

## Manual Review

V31 is accepted-forward because the equipment area has a clearer compact
armory-rack read: the sockets are smaller, less vertically dominant, visually
grouped into bay rows and empty targets read as actual slots.

Remaining director blockers:

1. the reference still has richer item silhouettes and stronger equipment-slot
   material polish
2. the right panel still depends heavily on runtime placeholder glyphs where the
   reference reads as real gear
3. bottom deck density was intentionally unchanged in this pass
4. map-stage director blockers from V30 remain unchanged

## Next Action

Do not continue with only equipment-slot alpha tweaks. Move to one of these
technique changes:

1. bottom deck/action-log density and command hierarchy
2. generated/authored equipment-slot placeholder art or richer chrome resources
3. map-stage composition/black-field balance if the first-screen map remains
   the dominant gap
